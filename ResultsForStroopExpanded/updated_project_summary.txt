Updated Stroop-Style VLM Experiment Summary
============================================================

This update extends the previous one-prompt CLIP experiment based on supervisor feedback.

New experiments added:
1. Prompt robustness testing with multiple prompt templates.
2. Standard text-meaning recognition control with black text.
3. Multilingual / cross-script Stroop testing with English and Chinese text.
4. Optional Qwen2.5-VL-3B black-box evaluation on a small subset.

Main 20k dataset:
- Total images: 20000
- Color classes: 10
- Conditions: congruent, incongruent, neutral, background_conflict
- Correct label: visual font color

Prompt robustness summary:
- ambiguous_written_in: overall accuracy=0.4451, incongruent word bias=0.9688, background-conflict word bias=0.9518
- font_color: overall accuracy=0.4780, incongruent word bias=0.9276, background-conflict word bias=0.9280
- ink_color: overall accuracy=0.4876, incongruent word bias=0.9050, background-conflict word bias=0.8592
- letters_color: overall accuracy=0.4505, incongruent word bias=0.9894, background-conflict word bias=0.9856
- minimal_control: overall accuracy=0.4358, incongruent word bias=0.9996, background-conflict word bias=0.9942
- word_oriented_control: overall accuracy=0.4355, incongruent word bias=1.0000, background-conflict word bias=1.0000

Text-meaning control overall accuracy: 1.0000

Multilingual summary:
- script=chinese, condition=background_conflict: accuracy=0.3020, word_bias_rate=0.0010
- script=chinese, condition=congruent: accuracy=0.8060, word_bias_rate=0.0000
- script=chinese, condition=incongruent: accuracy=0.7890, word_bias_rate=0.0190
- script=english, condition=background_conflict: accuracy=0.0850, word_bias_rate=0.8170
- script=english, condition=congruent: accuracy=1.0000, word_bias_rate=0.0000
- script=english, condition=incongruent: accuracy=0.1000, word_bias_rate=0.9000

Interpretation:
The prompt robustness experiment checks whether the observed word bias is due to ambiguous wording or persists with explicit ink/font-color prompts.
The text-meaning control checks whether the model can recognize written text meaning in a clean black-text condition.
The multilingual experiment tests whether word-bias changes across scripts such as English and Chinese.
The optional Qwen2.5-VL section allows the same Stroop-style black-box test to be extended to an instruction-following VLM.
